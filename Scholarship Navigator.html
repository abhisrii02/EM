How it works

One helper, four steps, until the money arrives.

1

Check

We confirm which schemes you can apply for and list the documents each one needs.

2

Prepare

We check income proof, the Aadhaar and bank link, and that names match across documents.

3

Apply

We sit with you while you fill the form on the official portal, in your own language.

4

Follow up

We track the status and send WhatsApp updates until the payment is credited.

Our promises

Rules we work by.

  • We never ask for your passwords or OTPs.
  • We use your documents only with your written consent.
  • We never promise approval. The government or institute decides.
  • You pay only if your scholarship is credited. Pilot terms are confirmed before you start.
  • We are an independent service, not linked to any government portal.
Fees

Who pays for what.

For families

Checking eligibility and documents is free. A small success fee applies only when the scholarship is credited. The amount is agreed with your school or NGO partner before you start.

For schools, colleges and NGOs

We help your students finish their applications, and plan to give you a simple status list for each student. A per-student support fee will be set during the pilot.

Questions

Before you start.

Is this a government service?

No. We are an independent pilot. Applications are always submitted on the official portals.

Do you guarantee a scholarship?

No. Approval is decided by the government or the institute. We help reduce mistakes and follow up.

What if my payment is stuck?

We check the status and the common causes, such as Aadhaar-bank linking or a name mismatch, then take it up with the institute or the portal.

Do you keep my documents?

Only with your consent, and they are deleted after your case is closed.
